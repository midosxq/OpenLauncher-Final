# OpenLauncher

**Your Home.**

OpenLauncher is an Android gaming launcher designed to bring a PC-like
gaming experience to Android devices, with an integrated keymapping
system for keyboard, mouse, and gamepad.

## Features

-   **Game Library** --- Organize, discover, and launch your Android
    games.
-   **Advanced Keymapper** --- Map keyboard, mouse, and gamepad controls
    to in-game actions.
-   **Mouse & Camera Control** --- Mouse aiming, camera sensitivity,
    cursor control, and Crazy Tap.
-   **Mapper Editor** --- Create and customize control layouts with an
    intuitive in-game editor.
-   **Game Overlay** --- Gaming performance overlay integration
    (planned).
-   **Recording & Highlights** --- Screen recording and screenshot
    features.
-   **Custom Hotkeys** --- Customize keyboard shortcuts for launcher and
    gaming functions.
-   **Performance Engine** --- Adaptive performance optimization for a
    smoother experience.

## Compatibility

-   Android 11--16 (target compatibility).
-   Rooted and non-rooted devices, subject to activation method support.
-   Designed with AOSP-based Android ROMs in mind.
-   No Google Mobile Services (GMS) dependency intended.

## Project Status

OpenLauncher is currently under development. Features and compatibility
are being refined across development phases.
